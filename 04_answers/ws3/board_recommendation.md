## Why these four

### Continue: Rosar Telecom Holdings
- **The largest fully healthy relationship in the portfolio.**
  - 21 Active projects, **none Past Due or on the Watchlist**.
  - Exposure-weighted internal risk rating **1.62 of 6**.
  - 14 projects already closed, **none at a loss**.
  - Health score 95/100.
- **Diversified:** 40 countries; the largest (Argentina) holds 22% of its exposure.
- **Sector view:** Telecom ranks 3rd of 5 industries on our own track record. GSMA's 2025 outlook supports
  prioritising connectivity, citing the mobile-internet usage gap and growth of Africa's mobile economy [S4, GSMA,
  March 2025].
- **Downside:** the stress test finds no Watchlist or rating 5–6 project to shock, so its expected credit loss stays
  at $0 under every scenario we run.

### Continue: Zansorzen Renewables Corp
- **The healthiest scorecard among large clients:** health score 96.7/100.
  - Exposure-weighted rating **1.41 of 6**.
  - 12 Active projects, none distressed.
  - 6 closed projects, none at a loss.
- **Diversified:** 26 countries; the largest (Bangladesh) holds 11% of its exposure.
- **Sector view:** Energy ranks **1st of 5** industries on our track record (A23). The IEA reports that clean-energy
  investment in emerging economies, and in Africa especially, is held back by the cost of capital, which is the gap
  IFC fills [S3, IEA, June 2025].
- **Downside:** $0 under every stress scenario.

### Reduce / review: Wenhargal Renewables Corp
- **The second-largest distressed exposure in the portfolio:**
  - **8 Past Due and 4 Watchlist** of 20 Active projects;
  - **70.6% of current exposure distressed**;
  - exposure-weighted rating **5.2 of 6**;
  - 17 Active projects rated 5–6;
  - 12 reviews overdue.
- One of its 4 closed projects was exited at a loss.
- **Sector view:** Energy is the industry we recommend prioritising, so this is a client-specific problem, not a
  sector one.
- **Downside:**
  - if its rating 5–6 projects default, expected credit loss rises by **$613M**;
  - if its Watchlist and rating 5–6 projects default and the remaining commitment is fully drawn first, it rises by
    **$1,008M**.

  That is why we recommend freezing the $1,185M undisbursed.

### Reduce / review: Bricor Finance Group
- **The largest distressed exposure in the portfolio:**
  - **4 Past Due and 6 Watchlist** projects;
  - **77.2% of current exposure distressed**, the highest of the four;
  - exposure-weighted rating **5.31 of 6**, the worst of the four;
  - 13 Active projects rated 5–6;
  - 11 reviews overdue.
- **Sector view:** Finance ranks **5th of 5** industries on our track record. The World Bank (June 2026) and the IMF
  (October 2026) both point to higher borrowing costs and rising public debt weighing on emerging-market lenders
  [S8].
- **Downside:**
  - +**$859M** expected credit loss if its rating 5–6 projects default;
  - +**$1,125M** in the severe case (Watchlist and rating 5–6 projects default, commitment fully drawn).

## How the four were chosen (and how robust the choice is)
1. **Bands:** every client gets a rule-based band from its own scorecard: CONTINUE (22 clients), MONITOR (246) or
   REDUCE_OR_REVIEW (131). The thresholds are in A15.
2. **Ranking within the bands:**
   - continue clients are ranked by size (exposure affected);
   - reduce clients are ranked by distressed exposure (Past Due + Watchlist disbursed).
3. **Sensitivity check:** re-ranking by health score and by a credit-heavy weighting keeps all four picks in the
   top 5.
4. **Team judgment, checked with the agents:**
   - The **stress-test agent** confirms that Bricor and Wenhargal are the two largest drivers of loss if the
     highest-risk projects default, and that the two continue clients add nothing to stressed losses.
   - The **capital-allocation agent** confirms the sector view: prioritise Energy, be cautious on Finance.
   - It also adds the one condition below.

## The condition: concentration in Sub-Saharan Africa
- **Sub-Saharan Africa holds 27.8% of IFC's current exposure,** above the 25% concentration limit (A23). It also
  ranks last of 6 regions on our track record.
- **All four clients have Sub-Saharan Africa as their main region,** although each is spread across many countries.
  About a third of each client's current exposure is in the region:
  - Rosar: $826M of $1,795M;
  - Zansorzen: $659M of $2,003M;
  - Wenhargal: $900M of $2,781M;
  - Bricor: $605M of $2,587M.
- **We therefore recommend:**
  - continuing with Rosar and Zansorzen **on the condition that new commitments do not raise Sub-Saharan Africa's
    share of current exposure**;
  - placing their new projects in South Asia and East Asia & Pacific, the two best-ranked regions that are not
    concentrated.

  Reducing Wenhargal and Bricor lowers the region's share.

## What this is not
- **Advice to the Credit Risk Committee, not an automatic decision.** Each recommendation is stored with
  `requires_human_review = true`.
- **The stress figures are what-ifs, not forecasts.** They use the brief's simplified expected-credit-loss model:
  Past Due = default, 60% of disbursed is lost (A11).
- **The outside research is paraphrased** from each publisher's summary (`ref_market_outlook`, retrieved
  16 November 2026). Verify against the full reports before quoting them to the Board.
- **The data is synthetic,** prepared for the challenge.
